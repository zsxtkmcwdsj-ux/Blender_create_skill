# Blender Skill Handoff

## Task
Create a research-grounded spatial concept for 北京建筑大学大兴校区7号楼2层学生公寓 from the supplied fire plan.

## Evidence
- The uploaded plan controls topology: a long central corridor, rooms 202-236, two stair cores, an east-middle elevator group, and a red evacuation route.
- Public sources establish BUCEA and Daxing campus context only. Exact Building 7 dimensions and interior finishes are not publicly verified.

## Spatial envelope gate
The deliverable must contain floor, walls, ceiling, real room-door openings, room 218 exterior window, stair cores and elevator doors. Do not submit a furniture-only vignette.

## Modeling scope
Model the corridor segment and repeated room shells, with a detailed representative room 218 containing two beds, desks, storage and a clear circulation path. Use inferred dimensions and record them in scene metadata.

## Delivery views
Render one human-scale room-to-corridor view and one elevated overview showing the floor-plan relationship. Export `.blend`, `.glb`, previews and an audit JSON.

## Truth labels
Use `fire-plan-based spatial concept` for the result. Treat room dimensions, ceiling height, windows, furniture and materials as inference, not measured evidence.

## Space contracts

- Room 218: floor, two side walls, windowed back wall, ceiling, corridor door with jambs and head above the door, then furniture.
- Corridor: continuous floor and ceiling, room-front walls, doors, readable width, exit route, and connections to stairs/elevator.
- Stairwell: enclosure, landing, two continuous flights, treads and vertical continuation.
- Elevator: shaft/core walls, landing, framed doors, cabin/shaft depth and vertical continuation.

Do not accept a space until its contract is visible in a cutaway or top-down check. Do not use furniture or a black block as evidence of architecture.
