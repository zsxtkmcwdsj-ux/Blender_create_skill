# Quality Gates

## Geometry

- Dimensions meet the approved tolerance. Default to `±1%`; use the user's stricter tolerance when specified.
- Vehicle hard dimensions and panel gaps use millimeter tolerances only when the references support them.
- No visible floating panels, unsupported members, intersecting glass, open seams, reversed normals, duplicate faces, zero-area faces, or unintended negative scale.
- Doors, hoods, tailgates, bumpers, windows, frames, seals, and supports have credible attachment boundaries.
- Continuous highlights cross major body surfaces without box-by-box discontinuities.
- Window openings include frame, seal, glass thickness, and interior depth.
- Stairs include steps, stringers, platforms, beams, braces, rails, and connections.

## Variation

- Buildings remain distinguishable without signs through facade, window, storefront, and structural differences.
- Repeated modules follow the reference rhythm and do not create obvious cloned arrays.
- Dirt, wear, wetness, and damage follow gravity, contact, drainage, and use instead of universal noise.

## Shading

- Use angle-controlled smoothing only.
- Keep intended sharp edges and bevels.
- Check highlights using a continuous strip-light environment or equivalent reflection test.
- Do not accept increased subdivision unless it improves a visible silhouette or curvature defect.

## Review views

For major assets, capture:

- front, rear, left, right, and top orthographic views;
- at least four 45-degree views;
- close-ups of critical joints and openings;
- neutral-light material check;
- final-environment check.

## Acceptance rule

Do not advance when a required close-up fails even if the wide shot looks acceptable. Record pass/fail evidence and the user decision in `stage-acceptance-template.md`.
