# China Landmark Exteriors

## Scope

Exterior-only conceptual Blender scene containing recognizable massing for selected Chinese landmarks:

- Temple of Heaven
- Forbidden City Hall
- Oriental Pearl Tower
- Shanghai Tower
- CCTV Headquarters
- Bird Nest Stadium
- Hong Kong-Zhuhai-Macao Bridge
- Suzhou Museum New

## Contract

- No interiors.
- Keep landmarks distinguishable by silhouette, material palette, and structural cues.
- Use native Blender primitives and editable modifiers only.
- Deliver `.blend`, `.glb`, and preview renders when Blender is available.

## Truth Status

Concept massing set, not survey-accurate reconstruction.
