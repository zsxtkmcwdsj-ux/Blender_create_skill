# 祈年殿 Realistic Exterior

Scope: exterior-only procedural reconstruction of the Hall of Prayer for Good Harvests.

Target features:

- three concentric white marble terraces;
- four cardinal stair approaches;
- circular balustrades, posts, and drainage spouts;
- round timber hall body with red columns and dark lattice panels;
- painted dougong bracket blocks below the lower eave;
- three stacked deep-blue glazed conical roofs with radial tile ribs;
- golden finial.

Truth status: reference-informed Blender model, not survey-accurate photogrammetry.
